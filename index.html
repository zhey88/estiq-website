FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What services does estiq offer?

We offer facials & skincare treatments, manicure & pedicure, nail art, eyelash extensions, eyebrow embroidery, hair removal, and head spa/scalp treatments — so you can get multiple treatments done in one visit instead of hopping between different salons.

Where are you located and what are your opening hours?

We're at 166 Joo Chiat Road, Singapore 427439, open daily from 10am to 9pm.

Do I need to book multiple appointments if I want more than one treatment?

No — just let us know what you'd like when booking (e.g. facial + nails, or lashes + head spa) and we'll schedule your visit accordingly. You can select multiple services in our booking form below.

How do I make a booking?

The easiest way is via WhatsApp — tap the booking button below or the WhatsApp icon, and let us know your preferred date, time, and services. We'll confirm availability with you directly.

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome depending on availability, but we recommend booking ahead via WhatsApp to guarantee your preferred time slot, especially on weekends.

Is parking available near estiq?

Street parking and nearby public car parks are available along Joo Chiat Road. The salon is also easily accessible by public transport.

What nail colors and designs do you have available?

Our nail color and design selection is regularly updated with new collections and seasonal trends, so please check with our staff in-salon or via WhatsApp for what's currently available.

Do you have any packages or promotions?

Yes, estiq regularly runs top-up packages and promotions (for example, top up $500 and get a bonus of $100, or more, depending on the current promotion). Offers change from time to time, so please contact our staff via WhatsApp for the latest packages and pricing.

Can I customize a treatment combo (e.g. nails + lashes + facial)?

Yes — many customers combine two or more treatments in a single visit. Let us know your preferred combination when booking and we'll arrange enough time for your appointment.

How long does each treatment take?

Duration varies by service — a full set of nails or lash extensions can take 1–2 hours, while facials and head spa treatments are usually 45–90 minutes. Our staff can advise on timing when you book.

Do you offer gift cards or vouchers?

Please check with our staff via WhatsApp or in-salon for current gift card and voucher options, as availability may vary.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and most major payment methods including PayNow and credit/debit cards. Do confirm with our staff if you have a specific payment method in mind.

Do I need to remove existing gel/acrylic nails before my appointment?

No need — our staff can remove existing gel, acrylic, or extensions for you as part of your visit. Just let us know when booking so we can allocate enough time.

Is estiq suitable for first-timers who have never had lash extensions or a head spa before?

Absolutely — our staff will walk you through the process, recommend suitable options, and answer any questions if it's your first time trying a treatment.

How far in advance should I book my appointment?

We recommend booking a few days in advance, especially for weekends or public holidays, to secure your preferred time slot.
