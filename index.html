estiq is an all-in-one beauty salon in Singapore — your one-stop destination for facials & skincare, manicure & pedicure, nail art, eyelash extensions, hair removal, and head spa treatments, all under one roof.

Welcome to estiq, Singapore's all-in-one beauty salon for beauty and wellness. Our team of highly skilled and experienced beauty specialists is passionate about helping you look and feel your absolute best — no need to visit multiple salons for different treatments.

Our studio is a cozy and inviting space that's designed to provide a relaxing and comfortable atmosphere for our clients. We use the latest equipment and high-quality products to ensure exceptional results every time.

As a true one-stop beauty salon, we offer a comprehensive range of services, including facial treatments and skincare, nail care (manicures, pedicures, gel nails, nail art), eyelash extensions, eyebrow services, hair removal, and rejuvenating head spa treatments. Our team stays up-to-date with the latest trends and techniques in the beauty industry to ensure that we're always providing you with the best service possible.

We believe that our clients deserve the best, and that's why we're committed to providing exceptional customer service. From the moment you walk into our studio to the time you leave, we'll make sure that you feel comfortable, pampered, and satisfied with your experience.
